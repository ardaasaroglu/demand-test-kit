How it works

  1. 1

    Add your plot and your seeds

    Photograph the seed packets you already have and say where you grow: a balcony, a back garden or an allotment.

  2. 2

    Get a plan every Monday

    See what to sow, plant out and harvest this week, timed to the frost dates for your area.

  3. 3

    Log what grew

    Two taps after each harvest. Next spring, your plan starts from the varieties that did well.
